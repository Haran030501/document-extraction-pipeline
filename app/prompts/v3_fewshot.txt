
Worked examples of tricky cases:

   parties: [{"name": "Jane Doe", "role": "appellant"}, {"name": "Acme Corp.", "role": "appellee"}]

2. Final line "AFFIRMED in part, REVERSED in part, and REMANDED." -> disposition: "affirmed_in_part"

3. "Before WARDLAW, BYBEE, and BRESS, Circuit Judges. Opinion by Judge Bress; Dissent by Judge Wardlaw" ->
   judges: ["Wardlaw", "Bybee", "Bress"], author_judge: "Bress"

4. "In Jones v. Smith, the jury awarded $5 million..." (a cited case) -> NOT a monetary amount for this case.

5. "PER CURIAM:" -> author_judge: null
